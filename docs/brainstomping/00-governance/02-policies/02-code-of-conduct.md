# Code of Conduct

Sets the behaviour expected of everyone who participates in resys-eshop project spaces.

**Links:** [Contributing](./01-contributing.md) · [Review policy](./03-review-policy.md) · [Governance index](../README.md) · [Spec](../../11-spec/README.md)

---

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
resys-eshop project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, as its baseline. The sections below state how that baseline applies here.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project, not just for ourselves.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any
  kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission.
- Dismissing or attacking a review comment instead of engaging with the technical
  substance.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Technical Disagreement

Disagreement about the design is expected and welcome; it is not a conduct violation.
Keep it about the work:

- Critique the change, not the person. Explain the problem and, where possible, propose
  an alternative.
- Ground arguments in the [design spec](../../11-spec/README.md)
  or an ADR under [../01-adr/](../01-adr/). Locked decisions are reopened by a new ADR,
  not by repeated argument in a pull request.
- If a discussion stalls, escalate through the review process in
  [Review policy](./03-review-policy.md) rather than by escalating tone.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issue
tracker, pull requests, code review, chat channels, and any in-person or virtual event
where someone is representing the project.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public
spaces, for example posting from an official account, acting as an appointed
representative at an event, or speaking on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to
the project maintainers responsible for enforcement. The current maintainer contact is
recorded in the repository's `CODEOWNERS` and in the project's published contact
channel; if you cannot find it, open a confidential report through the repository's
private security or conduct reporting mechanism.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences of
any action they deem in violation of this code of conduct:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning from maintainers, providing clarity around
   the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate.
   A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with
   the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the code
   of conduct, for a specified period. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or
   permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public
   communication with the community for a specified period. Violating these terms may
   lead to a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the
   community.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the code of conduct in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's
leadership.
